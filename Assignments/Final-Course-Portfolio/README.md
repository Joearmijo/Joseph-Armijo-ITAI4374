# Final-Course-Portfolio

This folder contains the final portfolio materials for ITAI 4374: Neuroscience as a Model for AI.

The portfolio represents the culmination of coursework, labs, experiments, projects, and neuroscience-inspired AI evaluations completed throughout the semester. The purpose of this portfolio is to demonstrate understanding of how biological intelligence and neuroscience principles influence modern artificial intelligence systems.

---

# Portfolio Contents

## Assignments
Written assignments exploring the relationship between neuroscience and artificial intelligence, including neural systems, learning mechanisms, and bio-inspired AI architectures.

## Labs
Hands-on neuroscience and AI labs involving:
- cortical column voting
- LIF neuron simulations
- retina and V1 visual processing
- memory and attention experiments
- spiking neuron models

## Midterm Project
A neuroscience-inspired AI audit analyzing ChatGPT through experiments involving:
- memory
- interference
- attention
- reasoning
- world models

## Final Project
An expanded neuroscience-based audit of ChatGPT evaluating:
- memory systems
- perception
- cognitive load
- selective attention
- reasoning
- ambiguity handling

The final project also explored brain-inspired redesign concepts for improving future AI systems.

# Course Reflection

This course helped me better understand the relationship between neuroscience and artificial intelligence. Through labs, experiments, and projects, I explored how biological systems process information and how those concepts influence modern AI systems such as neural networks, attention models, and reasoning architectures.

One of the most valuable aspects of the course was comparing biological cognition to artificial intelligence through real experiments. These activities demonstrated both the strengths and limitations of AI systems while showing how neuroscience principles may improve future artificial intelligence development.

Overall, this portfolio demonstrates my understanding of neuroscience-inspired AI systems and the connection between biological intelligence and machine learning technologies.
