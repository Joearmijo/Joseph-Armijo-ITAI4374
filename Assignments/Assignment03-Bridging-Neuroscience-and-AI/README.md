# Assignment03-Bridging-Neuroscience-and-AI

This assignment analyzed how neuroscience principles influence modern AI systems.

## Reflection

This assignment helped me better understand the relationship between biological neural systems and artificial intelligence architectures. While researching biological neurons and comparing them to artificial neural networks, I learned how structures such as dendrites, axons, synapses, and the soma inspired the design of modern neural networks.

One of the most interesting parts of the assignment was analyzing how different brain regions relate to AI architectures. I learned that the visual cortex inspired convolutional neural networks, the hippocampus relates to memory systems such as LSTMs and RNNs, and the hypothalamus has similarities to reinforcement learning systems. This showed me how neuroscience continues to influence the development of modern AI technologies.

The assignment also helped me understand the differences between biological and artificial systems. Biological neural networks are energy efficient, adaptive, and capable of continuous learning, while artificial systems are fast, scalable, and optimized mathematically. I found it interesting that AI systems still struggle to match the flexibility and efficiency of the human brain.

Overall, this assignment strengthened my understanding of neuroscience-inspired AI and showed how studying the brain can help improve future artificial intelligence systems through concepts such as modular learning, attention mechanisms, reinforcement learning, and neuromorphic computing.
